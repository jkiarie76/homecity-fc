# OFFICIAL APPOINTMENT ANNOUNCEMENT

Homecity FC is delighted to announce the appointment of **Fortune Otwori as the club's Team Manager**.

This appointment marks an important step in strengthening the club's leadership, coordination and commitment to excellence as we prepare for the challenges ahead.

We believe Fortune will bring dedication, teamwork, discipline and strong organisational skills to the role as we continue building a competitive team and nurturing football talent.

We call upon our players, technical bench, supporters, fans and the entire Homecity family to give him the necessary support and cooperation as he takes up his new responsibilities.

**Congratulations, Fortune Otwori!** We wish him every success in his new role as he serves the club and contributes to our shared vision.

**ONE TEAM. ONE FAMILY. ONE DREAM!**
